How to Use

1. Create Your Own Links

Click "Create New Link" from the menu or hero button. Fill in the details and generate your tracking link instantly.

2. Link Limit

Your plan determines how many active links you can create. When limit is reached, upgrade your plan to create more.

3. UID Checking

Enable UID checking to require visitors to enter their player UID before watching the reward video.

4. Popup Settings

Customize the entrance popup title, subtitle, button text, and image that visitors see before the video.

5. Traffic Analytics (48h Unique)

Visitors and video claims are counted on strict 48-hour unique basis. Duplicate clicks within 48 hours are excluded.

6. URL Shortener

Requires 3+ active links. Create short, clean links for your campaigns.

7. Plan Validity

Your plan duration decreases daily. Track remaining days from the home screen progress bar.

8. Renewal Plans

Open the menu and click "Renewal Plans" to view, compare, and purchase any plan. Your current plan cannot be re-purchased — only higher tiers are available.

Terms & Guidelines

1. Account Integrity

Users must register with legitimate credentials. Each link is private and exclusively managed by its owner.

2. Link Ownership

All links you create are permanently associated with your account and cannot be transferred.

3. Plan Expiry

Your plan duration decreases daily. When expired, all your links become inactive immediately. Renew to continue.

4. Content Policy

Do not create links for illegal, harmful, or misleading content. Violation leads to immediate account termination.

5. Data Privacy

Your visitor analytics are computed using IP + device fingerprint. We don't store personal visitor data beyond 48 hours.

6. Support

For any issues, contact support via WhatsApp with your account details.
