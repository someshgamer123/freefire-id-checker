Your plan determines how many links you can create. Fill details and generate tracking link instantly.

Terms & Guidelines

1. Account Integrity

Users must register via Google. Each link is private and exclusively managed by its owner.

2. Link Ownership

All links you create are permanently associated with your account.

3. Plan Expiry

When plan expires, all your links become inactive immediately. Renew to continue.

4. Content Policy

Do not create links for illegal or misleading content. Violation leads to account termination.

5. Data Privacy

Your visitor analytics are computed using IP + device fingerprint.
