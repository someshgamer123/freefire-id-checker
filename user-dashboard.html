📖 How to Use Your Dashboard

1. Account Access & Ownership

Sign in using your registered credentials. Only links authorized to your account profile will appear in your workspace.

2. Traffic & Claim Analytics

Track real-time performance of your active links, including Today Visits, 24-hour visitors, 7-day trends, and reward claims.

3. Selecting an Extension Plan

Choose from 7-day, 15-day, 30-day, 90-day, or 1-year plans. Selected packages will extend your validity duration accordingly.

4. Payment Verification

Scan the dynamic QR code with any UPI app. After sending payment, click "Request Admin Renewal" to notify the administrator for instant approval.

5. Manual Admin Assistance

Clicking "Request Admin Renewal" opens WhatsApp with pre-composed transaction details for direct review and extension.

📜 Terms & Guidelines

1. Account Integrity

Users must register with legitimate credentials. Each link is private and exclusively managed by its authorized holder.

2. Genuine Payment Submissions

Submission of fabricated, duplicate, or expired reference numbers will result in immediate account suspension.

3. Extension Policy

Payment verification extends your existing validity timeline. Unexpired days are preserved whenever an active link is renewed.

4. Support Desk

For any inquiries or manual verification requests, contact our Support desk with your link ID and receipt.
