📖 How to Use Your Dashboard

1. Account Access & Ownership

Sign in using your registered credentials. Only links authorized to your account profile will appear in your workspace.

2. Traffic & Claim Analytics (24h Unique)

Visitors and video claims are calculated on a strict 24-hour unique basis. Duplicate clicks by the same user within 24 hours are excluded.

3. Custom Date Selection

Filter data by specific single dates or custom date ranges using the Date Filter bar above the statistics.

4. Plan Limits

Each plan has a duration, link limit, and visitor limit. When you reach the visitor limit, your link expires automatically. Renew or upgrade to continue.

5. URL Shortener

Click the 3-line menu on the top-left and select "URL Shortener" to create short, clean links for your campaigns (requires 3 active links).

📜 Terms & Guidelines

1. Account Integrity

Users must register with legitimate credentials. Each link is private and exclusively managed by its authorized holder.

2. Extension Policy

Payment verification extends your existing validity timeline. Unexpired days are preserved whenever an active link is renewed.

3. Support Desk

For any inquiries or manual verification requests, contact our Support desk with your link ID and receipt.
