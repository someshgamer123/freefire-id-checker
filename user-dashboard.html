📖 How to Use Your Dashboard

1. Account Access & Ownership

Sign in using your registered credentials. For privacy and security, only links officially associated with your profile will appear on your dashboard.

2. Traffic & Claim Analytics

Track real-time performance of your active links, including Today Visits, 24-hour visitors, 7-day traffic trends, and verified reward claims.

3. Selecting an Extension Plan

Choose from 7-day, 15-day, 30-day, 90-day, or 1-year plans. Selected packages will reflect corresponding validity extension immediately upon confirmation.

4. Payment Verification

Scan the dynamic QR code with any UPI app. After completing payment, enter the 12-digit UPI reference (UTR) number into the verification box and submit.

5. Manual Request Assistance

If automated verification is disabled or pending, click "Request Admin Renewal" to open WhatsApp with your pre-filled transaction details for instant manual authorization.

📜 Terms & Guidelines

1. Account Integrity

Users must register with valid identification. Each link is private and exclusively managed by its authorized holder.

2. Genuine Payment Submissions

Submission of fabricated, duplicate, or expired 12-digit reference numbers will result in immediate account suspension.

3. Extension Timelines

Extensions are added to your existing validity date. Unexpired days are preserved whenever an active link is renewed ahead of time.

4. Support & Inquiry

For any discrepancies, contact our Support desk with your link ID and transaction receipt for resolution.
